OUR STORY

Made for people who keep clothes for years, not seasons.

Vanyara started with a simple complaint: most "timeless" clothing wasn't. We work with a small circle of mills and finishers, cut in small batches, and price things once — no seasonal markdowns designed to move product.

CRAFT

What we won't compromise on

Fabric first

We choose the cloth before we sketch the silhouette. If the fabric doesn't earn its place, the piece doesn't get made.

Small batches

Every style is cut in limited runs, so quality doesn't get diluted to hit a volume target.

Honest pricing

One price, always. No countdown timers, no invented "was" price next to it.

Get first access to new drops

One email, roughly once a month. No noise.
